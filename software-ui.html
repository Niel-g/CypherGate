Dashboard — Security Command Center

The Security Dashboard is the central hub of the CypherGate platform. It gives your security team a real-time, unified view of your organization's entire threat landscape — from live attack alerts to service health — all in one screen.

What You See

  • Security Health Score — a live composite score (0–100) calculated from network integrity, endpoint status, identity controls, and vulnerability exposure. A score of 92/100 means your environment is well-protected with minor open items.
  • Open Vulnerabilities — total count of unpatched CVEs found across your infrastructure, broken down by Critical, High, and Medium severity so your team knows where to act first.
  • Active Threats (CRM) — incidents currently in progress. Each threat is triaged by severity and assigned to a responder automatically.
  • Threats Blocked This Month — a cumulative count of attacks stopped by CypherGate's detection engine, showing the ROI of your security investment.

Security Health Breakdown

The donut chart and bar graph break your health score into four pillars: Network, Endpoint, Identity & Access, and Vulnerability. Each bar shows the coverage percentage for that domain so you can instantly identify the weakest link.

Live Activity Feed

A streaming log of security events happening right now — blocked intrusion attempts, PowerShell executions flagged as suspicious, backup completions, MFA logins, and patch deployments. Every entry is timestamped and tagged by severity color so your SOC analysts can triage at a glance.

Service Status Panel

Shows the real-time health of CypherGate's four core services: MDR, MFA, Vuln Scan, and Incidents. Green = Active, Amber = Degraded, Red = Down. This lets your team immediately know if a protection layer needs attention.

Threats Blocked — 7-Day Bar Chart

A color-coded bar chart showing Normal, Elevated, and Critical threat volumes day by day. Spikes help you identify patterns — for example, a surge on Friday evenings may indicate targeted weekend attacks on your organization.

MDR Monitor — Managed Detection & Response

Managed Detection & Response (MDR) is CypherGate's 24/7 threat hunting and response engine. The MDR Monitor screen gives your team full visibility into every active detection, investigation, and containment action being taken on your behalf.

What is MDR?

MDR combines human-led threat intelligence with automated detection tools. CypherGate's SOC analysts monitor your environment around the clock, hunting for threats that traditional antivirus and firewalls miss — such as fileless malware, lateral movement, and living-off-the-land attacks.

Key Elements on This Screen

  • Active Detections Feed — a live list of suspicious behaviors detected across your endpoints, network traffic, and cloud workloads. Each entry shows the affected host, detection rule triggered, and current status.
  • Threat Classification — detections are automatically classified as Informational, Low, Medium, High, or Critical using MITRE ATT&CK framework mapping.
  • Analyst Assignment — each critical or high detection is automatically assigned to a CypherGate analyst for manual review and guided remediation.
  • Containment Actions — when a threat is confirmed, analysts can isolate an endpoint, block an IP, revoke credentials, or kill a malicious process directly from this screen.

MITRE ATT&CK Integration

Every detection is mapped to a MITRE ATT&CK technique — the globally recognized framework for classifying adversary behavior. This tells your team exactly what the attacker was trying to do (e.g., T1059 — Command and Scripting Interpreter) so you can understand the attack chain and close gaps.

Why It Matters

Most breaches go undetected for an average of 207 days. MDR cuts this dwell time to under 15 minutes by combining continuous monitoring with instant analyst escalation — stopping attackers before they can move laterally or exfiltrate data.

Vulnerability Management — Find & Fix Before Attackers Do

The Vulnerability Management module continuously scans your infrastructure — servers, workstations, cloud instances, and applications — for known security weaknesses. It then prioritizes them by exploitability so your team fixes the highest-risk issues first.

How the Scanner Works

CypherGate's scanning engine uses a combination of authenticated network scans, agent-based endpoint scans, and cloud API integrations to discover every asset in your environment and check it against a database of over 200,000 known CVEs (Common Vulnerabilities and Exposures).

Risk Scoring (CVSS)

  • Critical (9.0–10.0) — remotely exploitable with no authentication required. Must be patched within 24 hours.
  • High (7.0–8.9) — significant risk, typically exploitable with minimal attacker effort. Patch within 7 days.
  • Medium (4.0–6.9) — requires specific conditions to exploit. Patch within 30 days.
  • Low (0.1–3.9) — limited impact. Patch in next scheduled maintenance window.

Asset Inventory

Every scanned device is automatically catalogued with its OS version, installed software, open ports, and running services. This creates a living asset inventory that updates in real time as new devices join your network.

Patch Recommendations

For each vulnerability, CypherGate provides specific remediation guidance — the exact patch to apply, the vendor advisory link, and workarounds if a patch is not yet available. Integration with patch management tools like WSUS, SCCM, and Intune allows one-click patch deployment directly from this screen.

Identity & Access — Zero Trust Identity Control

The Identity & Access Management (IAM) module enforces the principle of Zero Trust — never trust, always verify. It monitors every login, access request, and privilege escalation across your entire user base, catching account takeovers and insider threats before damage is done.

Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) Enforcement

CypherGate enforces MFA on all accounts — including service accounts and admin roles. The dashboard shows MFA adoption rate across your organization and flags any accounts that have bypassed or disabled MFA, creating a compliance gap.

What This Screen Shows

  • Active Sessions — all currently logged-in users with their device, location, IP address, and session duration. Anomalous sessions (e.g., login from a new country) are flagged automatically.
  • Privileged Access Monitor — tracks every action taken by admin and privileged accounts. Lateral movement attempts and privilege escalations trigger instant alerts.
  • Failed Login Heatmap — a geographic visualization of failed authentication attempts. Clusters of failures from a single region indicate a brute-force or credential-stuffing attack in progress.
  • Access Policy Violations — users attempting to access resources outside their role permissions are logged and can be auto-blocked based on your policy rules.

Single Sign-On (SSO) Integration

CypherGate integrates with Azure AD, Okta, Google Workspace, and LDAP environments. All identity events from these providers are ingested, normalized, and analyzed in real time within this module.

Why Identity is the #1 Attack Vector

Over 80% of breaches involve compromised credentials. Attackers don't break in — they log in. This module ensures that even if a password is stolen, the attacker cannot move through your environment undetected.

Incident Response — Contain, Investigate, Recover

The Incident Response (IR) module is CypherGate's war room. When a threat escalates beyond detection into an active breach or confirmed attack, this screen becomes the operational center for containment, forensic investigation, and full recovery.

Incident Lifecycle

  • Detection — an alert from MDR, a SIEM rule, or an analyst escalation creates a new incident ticket automatically.
  • Triage — the incident is classified by type (ransomware, phishing, insider threat, DDoS, etc.), severity, and affected systems.
  • Containment — affected endpoints are isolated from the network to prevent lateral movement. Malicious processes are terminated. Compromised accounts are suspended.
  • Investigation — forensic evidence is collected: memory dumps, process trees, network logs, and file hashes. The full attack timeline is reconstructed to understand entry point and attacker dwell time.
  • Eradication — malware is removed, backdoors are closed, and compromised credentials are rotated system-wide.
  • Recovery — affected systems are restored from clean backups. Services are brought back online in a controlled sequence.

Automated Playbooks

CypherGate uses SOAR (Security Orchestration, Automation and Response) playbooks that auto-execute containment steps the moment a confirmed breach is detected — cutting response time from hours to seconds. Playbooks are customizable per incident type.

Post-Incident Report

After every closed incident, CypherGate generates a detailed Post-Incident Report (PIR) including root cause analysis, full attack timeline, impacted assets, remediation steps taken, and recommendations to prevent recurrence. This report satisfies regulatory reporting requirements for GDPR, HIPAA, and ISO 27001.

Settings — Platform Configuration & Preferences

The Settings panel is the administrative control center for your CypherGate deployment. Security administrators use this screen to configure detection thresholds, manage integrations, control user permissions, and customize the platform's behavior to match your organization's policies.

Key Configuration Areas

  • Organization Profile — set your company name, industry vertical, regulatory compliance frameworks (PCI-DSS, HIPAA, ISO 27001, SOC 2), and geographic data residency preferences.
  • Detection Sensitivity — tune alert thresholds per module. Increase sensitivity to catch more subtle threats, or reduce it to minimize false positives in noisy environments.
  • Notification Rules — configure who gets alerted, at what severity level, and through which channel: email, SMS, Slack, Microsoft Teams, or PagerDuty.
  • Integrations — connect CypherGate to your existing stack: SIEM platforms (Splunk, QRadar), ticketing systems (ServiceNow, Jira), and cloud providers (AWS, Azure, GCP).
  • User & Role Management — create analyst accounts, assign role-based access (Read-Only, Analyst, Admin, Super Admin), and enforce session timeout and IP allowlist policies.
  • API Keys — generate and rotate API keys for custom integrations and third-party security tool connections.

Audit Log

Every settings change — who made it, what was changed, and when — is recorded in an immutable Audit Log. This is essential for compliance evidence and ensures no unauthorized configuration changes go unnoticed.

Data Retention Policy

Configure how long raw logs, incident records, and vulnerability reports are stored. CypherGate supports retention periods from 90 days to 7 years to meet different regulatory requirements.

Reports — Compliance, Insights & Executive Summaries

The Reports module transforms raw security data into actionable intelligence. It provides pre-built and custom report templates for every stakeholder — from technical SOC analysts who need raw event data, to executives who need a one-page risk summary, to auditors who need compliance evidence.

Report Types

  • Executive Security Summary — a monthly high-level overview of your security posture, top threats, SLA performance, and risk trend. Designed for C-suite and board presentations.
  • Incident Report — detailed forensic record of a specific incident including timeline, affected assets, attacker TTPs, and remediation steps. Used for regulatory reporting and post-mortems.
  • Vulnerability Report — full inventory of open vulnerabilities, CVSS scores, affected hosts, and remediation status. Exported for compliance audits.
  • Compliance Report — pre-formatted reports mapped to ISO 27001, SOC 2 Type II, PCI-DSS, HIPAA, and GDPR control requirements. Reduces audit preparation time from weeks to hours.
  • Threat Intelligence Report — a summary of threat actors, malware families, and attack techniques targeting your industry vertical during the reporting period.

Scheduling & Delivery

Reports can be scheduled automatically — daily, weekly, or monthly — and delivered to designated recipients via email. All reports are generated as PDF, CSV, or JSON depending on the audience.

Custom Report Builder

Use the drag-and-drop Custom Report Builder to combine metrics from different modules into a single report. Build a custom board report that shows vulnerability count, incident response time, threats blocked, and MFA adoption all in one document — tailored to your organization's KPIs.

Data Export & SIEM Integration

All report data can be exported via REST API or pushed directly into your SIEM (Splunk, QRadar, Microsoft Sentinel) for correlation with other data sources. Supports CEF, LEEF, and JSON log formats.
